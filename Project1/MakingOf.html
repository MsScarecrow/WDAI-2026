A lifetime of adventure

"REDs are doing Project Polaris, so we'll have Solaris".

Who would have thought that these words written on a friendgroup discord on a random Monday night would mark a beginning of such an adventure? A month after the inauguration of CD PROJEKT RED's "Dziewczyny w Grze!" mentoring programme 4th edition, the mentees from different fields of game development came together to create a game, bringing all their creative ideas to life. Knowing little about each other and game development in general seemed to mark a dead end for the team. Today, almost two years after that moment, after numerous ups and downs, changes in the team, demo on the way, and most importantly, a shared dream, it turned out to be wrong.

Read the story on how a group of friends turned a dream into something unforgettable.

The beginning

31st March 2025

12 friends decide to create a game that would allow all of them to showcase their gamedev skills.

First game design

April-May 2025

A concept board and presentation are created, and the vision of the project to come is set.

Full story structure and character concept arts

June 2025

The full story is drafted and divided into quests.

IRL meeting

25-26 October 2025

The whole team manages to meet in Warsaw during "Dziewczyny w grze!" halfway convention. A demo is planned to come out in December.

Major team changes

November 2025

As the demo gets developed, some members decide to leave the team and focus on their exams. The demo gets postponed indefinitely.

The new beginning

28th February 2026

After three months of slow development, the remaining team meets once again in Warsaw. The team gains two new members.

Game design 2.0

5th March 2026

Lead developers hold numerous meetings to scale the project down and set up goals for the upcoming year.

Steam Page Release

20th July 2026

After two months of intense work, a steam page gets published.

Official Game Announcement

25th November 2026

The game's teaser is published, marking the game's official announcement.

Demo Release

20th December 2026

Demo/early access of the game is planned to release on 20th December 2026.
