A mysterious disease ravages a scorched Polish city. A young girl sets out to find a cure to heal her beloved grandmother - but what will her past reveal?

Solaris is an immersive first-person action game set in an alternate, post-apocalyptic Soviet satellite Poland. You are Irka: a skilled rooftop runner and courier. Sneak, run and climb the roofs of the destroyed city to find a cure for your grandma infected by a mysterious disease called Solaris.

Explore a divided world - immerse yourself in the feuding districts of Bastion and Selev. Traverse the unique, alternate-history Soviet satellite Poland Poland from the highest of rooftops.

Uncover the past - dive into the ruins of a declining city to unveil the mysterious origin of Solaris. But beware: some truths are deadlier than any disease.

Master roof running - use a diverse set of first-person parkour skills. Leap, slide and sneak across the hostile rooftops where one wrong step can equal death.

Run or die - outrun and outsmart the dangerous creatures, turning a hostile environment to your advantage.
